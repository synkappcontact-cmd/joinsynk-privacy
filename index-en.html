Privacy Policy of Synk

Last updated: July 2026

Synk ("we", "our application") places great importance on protecting your personal data. This policy describes what data we collect, why, how it is used and protected, and what your rights are.

Data controller

The data controller is the operator of the Synk application, reachable at contact@joinsynk.app.

Use of data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Operate the application's features (matching, messaging, profile display, geolocation);
  • Process your purchases and manage your Synk Pro subscription or top-ups;
  • Ensure platform security (moderation, detection of abusive behavior, handling of reports);
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Measure and improve app usage (internal statistics, not shared for advertising purposes).

Your data is never sold to third parties and is not used for targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Subprocessors and hosting

Certain data is processed by technical providers acting on our behalf, strictly within the scope of their duties:

  • Supabase (database and authentication hosting, European Union region);
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase management);
  • Firebase (Google) (push notification delivery);
  • Resend (transactional email delivery);
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (payment processing through their respective systems).

These providers use your data solely to perform the requested services and are bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

Storage and security

Your data is hosted on servers located in the European Union (Supabase, Ireland region). Technical and organizational measures are in place to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

Retention period

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If your account is deleted, your personal data is permanently erased within a maximum of 30 days, except where a longer retention period is required by law (e.g. fraud prevention, accounting obligations).

Your rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: obtain a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • Right to rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure: request the deletion of your data;
  • Right to portability: receive your data in a structured, readable format;
  • Right to object: object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • Right to restriction: request the temporary suspension of processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@joinsynk.app. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country of residence (in France, the CNIL — www.cnil.fr).

Content reporting and moderation

Synk provides a reporting mechanism accessible directly within the application, allowing any user to report a profile, message, or behavior that violates our rules or the law. Each report is reviewed, and the subject of a moderation decision (suspension, content removal) is informed and may contest it by contacting us at contact@joinsynk.app.

Minimum age

Use of Synk is restricted to persons aged 18 and over. By signing up, you confirm that you have reached the legally required age in your country of residence. Any account found to belong to a minor will be suspended and deleted.

International transfers

Our technical providers may, in certain cases, be based outside the European Union (e.g. Google/Firebase). In such cases, data transfers are governed by appropriate safeguards recognized under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or functional changes. Any substantial change will be communicated to users via the application or by email.

Contact

For any question regarding this privacy policy or your personal data: contact@joinsynk.app
